# Privacy Policy for Task Evidence Lab

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Task Evidence Lab is a local-first application designed for sensitive task execution, quality audit trails, and engineering retrospectives.

## 1. Local Processing & Zero External Telemetry
- All task records, evidence hashes, timelines, and diffs are stored strictly on the local SQLite database.
- Zero analytics, trackers, or telemetry beacons exist in the codebase.

## 2. Link & Evidence Safety
- Evidence files copied into `data/evidence/` remain purely on local storage.
- External link checking is strictly user-initiated.

## 3. Data Ownership
- All task retrospectives and evidence data belong entirely to the user.
